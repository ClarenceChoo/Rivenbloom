# Rivenbloom gameplay trailer

The revised trailer follows an unhurried journey from the village into sword
combat and the Pallid Cantor encounter. Four continuous gameplay takes replace
the previous nine clips. Each combat take holds long enough to show attacks and
responses. The boss changes phase inside the same recording.

The edit runs 36.2 seconds to allow longer holds. It has three transitions between
gameplay scenes, followed by the title card. Gameplay uses the native camera and
normal playback speed. The final title reads "Rivenbloom" and "Follow the silent
chimes home." There is no narration or release-date claim.

The timing and audio source ranges are in `composition-brief.md`. The soundtrack
is the new original `A Voice Through Glass` cue, mixed with captured game effects.
The original 25-second export is retained for comparison.
