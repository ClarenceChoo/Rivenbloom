# Fresh production route checked on October 7

This is a QA route, not a required optimal play style. It was played in a new Chromium profile against the built production app using keyboard controls and visible UI. The production developer bridge was absent. The observation helper only recorded Canvas draw calls and read IndexedDB; it never changed game or save state. The route included pauses, resumed commands, checkpoint reloads and one death in Echo Pool.

1. Begin Journey 1, accept Sela's request, and enter Brackenreach. Rest at the Trailhead, open the Wayfarer Cache, fight the Briars, break the root knot with a heavy attack, and light the memorial lantern. Visit Split Cedar for the Resin Cache.
2. Open the Listening Arch shortcut and Survey Cache. Continue through Singing Hollows, light its lantern, collect the Heart Petal, rest at Root Memory, and recover the memory. A bird interrupted the first Heart Petal approach and caused a death; checkpoint recovery preserved earned caches. The next approach succeeded.
3. Return to Piri, learn Aegis, and purchase three Sunmoss Draughts. Complete the timed Dash Trial using the ladder and three plates. Rest at Reliquary Verge.
4. Approach close enough to wake the Sentinel. Wait for its attack telegraph, dash past it, turn and use a three-hit light combo. Five successful combos and two Draughts defeated it. Verify the core before leaving; a sleeping or off-screen actor's absence is not victory. Return to Orin and purchase the earned reforge.
5. Enter Rootglass, open the archive's Index Chest, use the Index Seal and Rootglass Forge, then enter the East Lens Vault. Align its three lenses in order and collect the Wellspring Seed. Continue to Flooded Stacks, fight the guard, break the silt wall, recover the folio, and light the third lantern.
6. Return the folio to Sela and the memorial lights to Piri. Equip Quiet Step and buy three more Draughts. The previous remaining Draught was used in the dungeon. Final earned equipment: weapon level 2, 120 maximum health, 56 maximum mana, Aegis, Dash and Resonant Pulse.
7. Rest at Gallery. Pulse its memory and breath lenses in order, respecting the cooldown, then use the song seal. The first pulse was cast from outside body overlap but within the visible pulse radius, exercising the corrected puzzle activation. Enter Hollow Choir and rest.
8. Fight the Cantor with close three-hit combos. At the phase change, select Resonant Pulse and awaken both arena lenses. Return to the exposed heart and finish the fight. One of the three remaining Draughts was used; victory left 59 health and two Draughts. Verify exactly one sigil, the return-to-Sela objective and the durable defeat flag.
9. Rest and reload. Verify no living Cantor, no duplicate reward and an open return route. Return through the dungeon and Hollows, use the Arch shortcut, then choose **Mark the song returned** with Sela.
10. Verify **The song returns**, **Credits** and **Journey complete**. Choose **Continue exploring**, reload, and speak with Sela again. All three quests remain complete, the sigil quantity remains one, and credits do not replay.

The completed save recorded roughly 11.4 minutes of active simulation time; known-route automation, pauses and reloads make this unsuitable as a first-play duration measurement. Two first-time testers and target-device profiling remain the next qualification steps.
